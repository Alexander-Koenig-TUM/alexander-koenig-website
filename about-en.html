About

Researcher, founder, professor.

I do research on robotics and embodied AI, and I led a robotics company for eight years. Today I help companies understand the state of the technology and find their own way in.

FOR COMPANIES

Orientation from two perspectives.

At my chair I see what robotics and embodied AI can do in the lab and how far the road to everyday use still is. As a founder I experienced what it takes to turn that into a product. Together, the two are the basis of my keynotes and my advisory work.

I work with boards, executives and strategy teams: in workshops, on entry strategies, in startup challenges, on technology due diligence for investors and as an ongoing sparring partner. The questions come from logistics and manufacturing, from home and service, from medicine and care. The security of connected robots is always part of it.

REACTIVE ROBOTICS

Eight years as founder and CEO.

I founded Reactive Robotics in 2015, a robotics company that grew out of my research at ETH. The robot mobilises patients in intensive care units: a classic robotics problem in a demanding regulatory environment.

Over eight years we took the system through every stage into use: research, product, software, CE marking, FDA clearance, sales in six countries. Alongside that, teams of up to 50 people, eight-figure funding and twelve international patents.

What remains from that time is less a single product than a perspective: how robotics research becomes a company, which disciplines have to work at the same time (product, regulation, funding, sales) and where it really gets difficult.

CHAIR

Research on robotics and embodied AI.

At the Chair of Robotics and Systems Intelligence at TUM, 37 researchers and doctoral candidates work on the foundations of what will be everyday reality in five to ten years: embodied AI, reliable robotics and humanoid systems.

With GARMI we are developing a service humanoid on which we test grasping, navigation, perception and safety under everyday conditions, among other things for assisting older people. As a fellow of the rel-AI Konrad Zuse School of Excellence I work on reliable AI: systems whose decisions hold even when a lot is at stake.
